# About

## Whoami

I'm just a guy who likes digital forensics and DFIR.

## About this site

This is my public notebook for CTF write-ups, forensic articles, and commands I would rather not look up a fourth time. Most pages start as notes to myself. I publish them in the hope that they save someone else a bit of time, too.

- **[Write-ups](../writeups/index.md)**: CTF and lab challenges, with my reasoning and the steps I took to solve them.
- **[Articles](../articles/index.md)**: in-depth explanations, practical labs, shorter articles, and references. The index distinguishes their depth.
- **[To-Do List](../to-do-list.md)**: To-do list for upcoming publications and tasks to be completed.

## Language and tooling

I write in English and sometimes use LLMs to refine wording or translate my French notes. The investigations, reasoning, and conclusions are mine. If a model contributes beyond wording, I mention it on the page.

## Ground rules

- Opinions are mine and do not represent any employer, past or present.
- Everything is provided as-is. Read commands before running them against
  something you care about.
